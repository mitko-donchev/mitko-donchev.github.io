Description

Short

Waybound is an action-RPG roguelike built around one road out of a village — woods, narrows and a skeleton camp — with fast bow combat, a leap that buys you room, and runs that reshape every time you fall.

Long

There is a village, and there is one road out of it. The gate stands open and nobody is guarding it. Take the road and find out what that is worth.

Fast, readable bow combat: nock, loose, and a leap that buys back the distance you just gave away. Skeletons come in pairs, the narrows give you nowhere to stand, and the thing at the far end does not flinch when you chip it.

Part action-RPG, part roguelike. Runs reshape themselves every time you fall, and each attempt teaches you something the last one charged you for.

Built from the ground up in Godot with GDScript by a small team making the game we always wanted to play — in the open, with the players who care about it as much as we do.

Features

  • Bow combat you can read. Nock, loose, and a short leap that buys back the distance you just gave away.
  • Encounters that punish noise. Wake one skeleton within seven metres of another and both of them come.
  • Ground that takes your options away. The narrows squeeze the road to fourteen metres. No room to kite.
  • Runs that reshape. Each attempt teaches you something the last one charged you for.
  • A book you carry. Not a menu — a real object that keeps a record of the road behind you.
